Rate My TOS is a Chrome extension that evaluates and grades terms of service agreements, empowering users to make informed privacy decisions.

<b>Highlights:</b>

- Co-developed a Chrome extension to evaluate and grade terms of service agreements based on criteria such as user data privacy, user rights and ownership, and third-party data sharing
- Constructed a responsive front-end using React.js and Tailwind CSS, ensuring clear and effective presentation of TOS ratings
- Implemented integration with Google Gemini to analyze web-scraped terms of service and generate actionable insights
