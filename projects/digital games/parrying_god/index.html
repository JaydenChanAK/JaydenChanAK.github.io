Parrying God

Designer and Developer
Sekiro meets Smash Bros.
January - May 2025

Parrying God is a high-intensity 3rd person multiplayer smash-like fighting game where powerful abilities, devastating combos, and godlike enhancements determine the victor. Players battle in evolving arenas, wield various weapons, and choose a patron deity to appease. Master the art of parrying to overcome your foes, build favor, and transform into an avatar of your patron. In Parrying God, every clash is a test of one’s skill, and only the most skilled warriors survive.

Experience Goals:

  1. Players should experience a power fantasy.
  2. Players should feel a sense of competition.

Design Goals:

  1. Cultivate competitive gameplay.
  2. Create an in-depth parrying system to reward skillful gameplay.
  3. Design original gameplay that stands out from the genre.

Key Features:

  1. Seamless online multiplayer powered by Steamworks and Mirror integration.
  2. Smash-inspired knockback system based on damage percentage for high-stakes combat.
  3. A deep and responsive combat system featuring parries, dodges, powerful abilities, intricate combos, and game-changing god forms.
  4. Dynamic combo mechanics with real-time, adaptive UI feedback for each input.
  5. Robust character customization including deity selection, weapon loadouts, and cosmetics.
  6. Evolving battle arenas that dynamically shrink, increasing intensity as matches progress.
